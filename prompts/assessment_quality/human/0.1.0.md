# Assessment Quality Rating Protocol 0.1.0 (Development Draft)

Status: development draft. Calibrate on development examples before freezing or using for confirmatory ratings.

## Purpose

Rate one displayed rubric-based assessment of one reflection. Judge the assessment, not its associated feedback. Do not infer the generation condition, model, or author, and do not compare it with another output during scoring.

## Materials shown

The packet provides the segmented reflection with segment IDs, the rubric and anchors, and one assessment containing dimension scores, justifications, and cited evidence segment IDs. It does not show gold annotations, expert reference scores, human feedback, condition, model, or intermediate analysis.

## Rating scale

For each criterion choose an integer from 1 to 5, or select `unable_to_judge` and explain why. 1 = very poor/materially wrong; 2 = poor/major issues; 3 = mixed/partly sound; 4 = good/minor issues; 5 = excellent/consistently sound and well-supported.

## Criteria

- Rubric alignment: Do the dimension scores reflect the supplied rubric anchors and evaluate the whole reflection rather than mechanically applying segment labels?
- Score defensibility: Are the scores plausible given the reflection and the rubric? Distinguish this quality judgment from agreement with any hidden gold score.
- Evidence support: Do cited segment IDs exist and support the corresponding judgment? Are important claims unsupported or contradicted?
- Justification quality: Is the rationale clear, dimension-specific, and logically connected to the cited evidence and rubric?

## Rater guidance

Use `unable_to_judge` only when the packet lacks enough information for that criterion, not merely because you disagree with the assessment. Add an overall comment for concerns that do not fit a criterion. Do not infer missing evidence or expose condition/model guesses.

These are quality judgments, not reference rubric scores. Assessment accuracy against independent expert references is analyzed separately.
