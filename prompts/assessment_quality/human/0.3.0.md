# Assessment Quality Rating Protocol 0.3.0 (Development Draft)

Rate one assessment against the segmented reflection and rubric. Associated feedback, condition, model, intermediate analysis, and hidden reference scores are not shown. Keep the three criterion scores separate. These are assessment-quality judgments, not learner rubric scores or hidden-gold agreement.

## Score-Rubric Fit

Is the assigned score plausible given the reflection and rubric anchors? This combines rubric application and score defensibility; do not score them separately.

1. The score substantially conflicts with the reflection or rubric anchors.
2. The score is broadly plausible, but its placement within the rubric is questionable.
3. The score is defensible given the reflection and rubric anchors.

## Evidence Support

Does the cited evidence actually support the assessment's claims? Judge the validity and relevance of evidence, not how clearly the rationale explains it.

1. Central judgments rely on absent, irrelevant, or contradictory evidence.
2. Some evidence supports the judgments, but a meaningful gap remains.
3. Cited evidence exists and supports the central judgments.

## Justification Quality

Does the rationale clearly explain how evidence and rubric lead to the score? Relevant evidence can still be explained poorly. Judge clarity and reasoning separately from evidence validity.

1. Rationales are missing, incoherent, or fail to explain the judgments.
2. Rationales partly explain the judgments but are vague or weakly connected.
3. Rationales clearly connect dimension judgments, rubric, and reflection evidence.

## Rating procedure

Choose integer 1, 2, or 3 for each criterion. Select `unable_to_judge` with a null score and an explanation only when necessary information is missing. It is not the middle score or a substitute for disagreement. Add criterion or overall comments where useful. Do not guess hidden metadata or compare another output during scoring. Do not average the criteria into one overall score.

Calibrate on development examples before confirmatory use.
