# Assessment Quality Rating Protocol 0.2.0 (Development Draft)

Rate one assessment against the segmented reflection and rubric. Associated feedback, condition, model, intermediate analysis, and hidden reference scores are not shown. Keep the four criteria independent; these are assessment-quality judgments, not learner rubric scores or hidden-gold agreement.

## Criterion-specific anchors

Rubric alignment:
1. Materially misapplies rubric dimensions or anchors.
2. Partly aligns, with a limited inconsistency or unclear anchor application.
3. Consistently applies the rubric dimensions and anchors to the whole reflection.

Score defensibility:
1. Scores are substantially implausible given the reflection and rubric.
2. Scores are partly defensible but a limited discrepancy remains.
3. Scores are plausible and defensible given the reflection and rubric.

Evidence support:
1. Central judgments rely on absent, irrelevant, or contradictory evidence.
2. Some evidence supports the judgments, but a meaningful gap remains.
3. Cited evidence exists and supports the central judgments.

Justification quality:
1. Rationales are missing, incoherent, or fail to explain the judgments.
2. Rationales partly explain the judgments but are vague or weakly connected.
3. Rationales clearly connect dimension judgments, rubric, and reflection evidence.

## Rating procedure

Choose integer 1, 2, or 3 for each criterion. Select `unable_to_judge` with a null score and an explanation only when necessary information is missing. It is not the middle score and does not mean disagreement. Add criterion or overall comments where useful. Do not guess hidden metadata or compare another output during scoring.

Calibrate on development examples before confirmatory use.
