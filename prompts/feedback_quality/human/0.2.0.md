# Feedback Quality Rating Protocol 0.2.0 (Development Draft)

Rate one feedback output against its reflection and rubric. Condition, model, gold scores, and intermediate analysis are hidden. Judge the two criteria independently; do not average them into an overall score.

## Correctness

Judge whether claims accurately represent the reflection and rubric, without invented events, unsupported interpretations, or misleading claims. Explicit citations are not required. Correctness is not completeness: omitting a useful observation is not itself an error.

1. Major inaccuracies or misleading interpretations undermine the feedback.
2. Mostly accurate, but contains a limited error, overstatement, or questionable interpretation.
3. Accurate and defensible against the reflection and rubric, with no material inaccuracies.

## Developmental usefulness

Judge whether the feedback offers a relevant, meaningful way forward. A concrete suggestion or a productive question can be useful; both are not required. Specificity, actionability, and reflection potential guide this single judgment, not separate scores. Do not lower this score solely for a factual error already scored under correctness; consider whether the proposed direction is relevant and productive.

1. Generic, irrelevant, or provides no meaningful direction.
2. Relevant and partly useful, but the direction is vague or underdeveloped.
3. Offers a clear, relevant next step or productive direction for deeper reflection.

## Unable to judge and comments

For each criterion choose 1, 2, or 3. Use `unable_to_judge` with a null score and a reason only when the packet lacks information needed to judge. This is not the middle category or a substitute for disagreement. Optional span comments use exact zero-based, end-exclusive offsets in the displayed feedback item; source reflection links are separate. Optional tags: unsupported, inaccurate, vague, actionable, strength, concern, other. Add an overall comment when needed. Do not guess hidden conditions or compare other outputs while scoring.

Development pilot only: calibrate these anchors before confirmatory use. These are feedback quality ratings, not learner rubric scores.
